Chapter one

Why people choose
a reset.

Life is fast, demanding and often excessive. Over time, constant pressure and constant eating can leave us feeling heavy, sluggish and out of balance.

Processed food, oversized portions, alcohol, caffeine, late nights, stress and irregular routines all add up. Many people notice it as low energy, weight gain, bloating, cravings, restless sleep — or simply feeling out of rhythm with themselves.

The Lemon Detox Cleanse is a structured pause from regular eating and daily excess. For a defined period, solid food is replaced with the Lemon Detox drink. You follow one simple routine instead of making hundreds of food decisions a day.

That pause is the point. It interrupts automatic habits — the snacking, the grazing, the eating out of boredom — and gives you a clear line to start again from.

For many people, this is the opportunity to reset — physically, mentally and emotionally — and to come away with renewed discipline, clearer focus and a stronger sense of control around food.

Chapter three

A short, structured
cleanse.

The Lemon Detox Cleanse replaces regular meals with the Lemon Detox drink for a defined period — 7 to 14 days — supported by a simple morning and evening routine.

It isn't a long-term eating plan, and it isn't a water fast. The drink provides natural sugars and hydration so you can keep up your normal day. What it asks for is preparation, consistency and a few days of discipline.

7-day program

7-Day Focused Cleanse

Seven structured days for a fast, focused reset. Ideal for first-timers.

Most popular

14-Day Extended Cleanse

Two weeks for a deeper reset and longer-lasting habits. Ideal for returning cleansers.

In your Reset Box
  • Madal Bal® Natural Tree Syrup
  • Senna herbal tea for the evening
  • Natural sea salt for the morning flush
  • This program guide

You'll also need fresh lemons, cayenne pepper and water — see the shopping list on page 07.

Chapter four

The Lemon Detox drink.

The foundation of the program — four simple ingredients that keep you going while you take a break from solid food.

Ingredient 01

Madal Bal® Natural Tree Syrup

A blend of maple and palm syrups — the energy source of the drink, providing natural sugars through the day. Included in your Reset Box.

Ingredient 02

Fresh lemon juice

Always freshly squeezed, never bottled. Adds vitamin C and the drink's bright, clean taste.

Ingredient 03

Cayenne pepper

Just a pinch — enough for gentle warmth without heat.

Ingredient 04

Water

Fresh or filtered water brings the syrup, lemon and cayenne together into a drink you can sip all day.

Also part of the routine

Senna herbal tea in the evening and the salt-water flush in the morning are not part of the drink. They support the daily routine and are explained on page 08.

Chapter five

How to make
the drink.

Make the full day's batch each morning — or the night before — so it's ready wherever you are.

Recommended

Daily batch · about 2 litres

  1. 140 ml Madal Bal® Natural Tree Syrup
  2. 140 ml freshly squeezed lemon juice (about 3½ lemons)
  3. 2–3 pinches cayenne pepper
  4. Water to make up to 2 litres (about 1.7 litres)
Single glass

One glass

  1. 20 ml Madal Bal® Natural Tree Syrup (1 Australian tablespoon)
  2. 20 ml freshly squeezed lemon juice (about ½ lemon)
  3. A small pinch of cayenne pepper
  4. At least 250 ml water
Preparation notes
  • Use fresh lemons — never bottled juice.
  • Stir or shake before each glass; the ingredients settle.
  • Enjoy cold, at room temperature or warm — never boiled.
  • Once opened, keep the tree syrup in the fridge.

Chapter six

The daily routine.

Three simple steps, the same every day. Within a day or two it becomes second nature.

I · Morning

Salt-water flush (or senna tea)

On an empty stomach, dissolve 2 level teaspoons of natural, uniodised sea salt in 1 litre of warm water and drink it. Prefer not to, or have high blood pressure, a heart or kidney condition? Have a cup of senna herbal tea instead. Then mix the day's Lemon Detox drink.

II · Through the day

Sip the drink steadily

Have a glass whenever you feel hungry — usually six to nine glasses a day. The full daily batch travels easily to work.

Hydration

Plain water in between

On top of the drink, have 1–2 litres of plain water through the day. Herbal teas such as peppermint, chamomile or rosehip are fine too.

III · Evening

Senna herbal tea, then rest

Once the day's drink is finished, have a cup of senna herbal tea before bed. Use as directed on the packet and only for the duration of the cleanse. Aim for a full night's sleep.

Gentle walking and stretching are a good complement. Save intense training for after the cleanse.

Which way
suits you?

Both programs can be followed three ways. The routine is the same — the difference is how many meals you replace.

Deepest reset

Full Cleanse

Every meal is replaced with the drink.

  • Breakfast: 2–3 glasses
  • Lunch: 2–3 glasses
  • Dinner: 2–3 glasses
Most flexible

Relaxed

Breakfast and dinner are replaced; lunch is a light, healthy meal.

  • Breakfast: 2–3 glasses
  • Lunch: light meal
  • Dinner: 2–3 glasses
Ease in, ease out

50/50

Relaxed, then Full Cleanse, then Relaxed again.

  • Days 1–3: Relaxed
  • Days 4–6: Full
  • Days 7–9: Relaxed

Chapter eight

Before you start.

Who the cleanse isn't for

Please don't do the Lemon Detox Cleanse if you are pregnant or breastfeeding; under 18; living with diabetes, heart, kidney or liver disease, or high or low blood pressure; recovering from surgery or illness; or living with, or have a history of, an eating disorder. If you take prescription medication, speak with your doctor first.

If at any point you feel faint, dizzy, unwell or notice heart palpitations, stop the cleanse, eat normally and seek medical advice if symptoms continue.

The week before
  • Pick a start date in a calm week — avoid big events.
  • Cut back on coffee, alcohol and processed food gradually.
  • Eat lighter meals for the last two days.
  • Buy your lemons and cayenne pepper.
  • Tell the people you live with — support helps.
What to expect
Days 1–2Adjusting. Hunger and mild headaches are common, especially for coffee drinkers.
Days 3–4Settling. Most people find the routine gets easier.
Days 5–7In rhythm. Many feel lighter, clearer and more in control.
Week 2Habit. The routine feels effortless.

Everyone is different — this is what participants commonly describe, not a promise of how your body will respond.

Chapter nine

Coming off
the cleanse.

How you finish matters as much as how you start. Ease back into food over two or three days rather than going straight back to your usual meals.

Day 1 after

Liquids first

Fresh juices, vegetable soup or broth. Keep sipping water.

Day 2 after

Fresh and light

Add fruit, salads and steamed vegetables. Small portions, eaten slowly.

Day 3 onward

Back to balance

Reintroduce lean protein and whole grains, then your normal balanced diet.

Keeping your results
  • Keep the water habit — 1.5–2 litres a day.
  • Keep portions smaller than before the cleanse.
  • Hold on to the habits you broke: grazing, late-night snacking, sugary drinks.
  • Reintroduce coffee and alcohol slowly — or not at all.
Doing it again

Many people repeat the cleanse once or twice a year as a planned reset. Leave at least four weeks between full programs.
